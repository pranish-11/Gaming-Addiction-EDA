# Gaming Habits & Behavioral Metrics EDA

Exploratory data analysis (EDA) of gaming habits, behavioral metrics, and psychological impact using Python, Pandas, and Seaborn.

## Key Findings & Insights

- **High Daily Playtime:** The average daily playtime across the cohort is **6.15 hours**, with extreme cases reaching nearly **12 hours** per day.  
- **Stress & Mental Health:** Participants show elevated average stress scores (**6.82/10**), indicating strong positive correlations between excessive screen/gaming hours and psychological strain.  
- **Financial Monetization:** Average monthly in-game spending sits at **$41.12**, with outliers spending upwards of **$182.60**, highlighting intensive monetization patterns.  
- **Distribution & Anomalies:** Statistical profiling revealed clusters of heavy users facing synchronized spikes in dopamine dependency metrics and drops in productivity scores.

## Technologies & Libraries Used
- **Python** (Core data processing)
- **Pandas & NumPy** (Data cleaning, manipulation, and summary statistics)
- **Seaborn & Matplotlib** (Data visualization and distribution plots)

## Dataset Overview
- **Total Samples:** 250 rows
- **Features:** 49 columns covering demographics, gameplay habits, mental health indicators, and behavioral scores.
